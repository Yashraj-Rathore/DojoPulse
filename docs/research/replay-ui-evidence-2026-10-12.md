## Correction and next evidence

The earlier instruction to download before playback was too broad. Own My Replays & Tips
items can be approached through the shown Play Replay action. Do not require a separate
download step for an already accessible replay or conflate an in-game native save with MP4
export. The [community recorder README](https://github.com/Cathesilta/tekken8_oneshot_replays)
describes its own already-downloaded workflow; it is not a current universal menu contract.
Absence of an export button in these screenshots does not prove no other export/download
operation exists, nor that external acquisition is impossible.

For a personal developer capture check on the Steam version, Valve documents its built-in
[Game Recording](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/23B7-49AD-4A28-9590): select manual
recording in Steam settings, record playback, make a clip in Recordings & Screenshots and
export it as a video file. This is a documented local candidate; its actual output profile,
Tekken compatibility, full-match bytes and DojoPulse admission have not been tested here.
No recorder installed, settings changed, video created or export/private delivery occurred.

The next developer evidence is one uninterrupted original-speed recording with the displayed
